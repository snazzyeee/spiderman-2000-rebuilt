# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good faith, and keep
discussion focused on the work. Harassment, personal attacks and discriminatory
language are not tolerated.

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer through the
repository's **Security → Report a vulnerability** form (choose a title starting
with "Conduct:"), or by contacting the maintainer through their GitHub profile.
Maintainers may remove comments, close issues or pull requests, or block
participants who violate this code.
